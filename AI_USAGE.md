# AI Usage

**Tool:** An OpenAI GPT-based assistant.

**Purpose:** Assistance with parts of the Python code and report preparation, reviewing explanations, and arranging figures, tables, and page layout in the final PDF.

**Verification:** The checks covered audio-file counts, separation of speaker IDs across data splits, consistency between numerical tables and reported values, and visual inspection of the generated figures and PDF pages.

**Scope and responsibility:** These checks focus on reproducibility, numerical consistency, and presentation. AI suggestions serve as supporting material; responsibility for the analysis, its interpretation, and the submitted work remains with the author.
